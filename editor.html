Quick guide
  • Wall: drag a rectangle on the grid.
  • Select: click loot, enemies, or puzzles to edit their settings.
  • Switch: make a switch barrier, then choose it as a target and place a switch beside a wall.
  • Navigate: use room names, or click a door with Select. Expand the arrow beside a room to see its dungeons. Searching also reveals matching dungeon rooms.
  • Large rooms: scroll the canvas or use Fit.
  • Undo: Ctrl / Cmd + Z. Redo with Shift + Z.
  • Erase: drag to remove objects or wall tiles. Deleting a room door also removes its connected room tree after confirmation.
